What You'll Learn

Professional AI curriculum built for Cambodia

Our flagship 3-hour intensive covers everything from prompt basics to institutional AI governance — with Cambodia public sector examples throughout.

Why AI Prompts Matter + Prompt Anatomy Blueprint

The hidden costs of poor prompts. The 5-component blueprint: Context, Task, Format, Constraints, Examples.

S2

CASTROFF Framework — Professional Standard

8-dimension prompt engineering standard: Constraints, Audience, Structure, Tone, Role, Output, Focus, Function.

Advanced Strategies: CoT, ToT & Pipelines

Chain-of-Thought for complex reasoning. Tree-of-Thought for policy options. Multi-step prompt pipelines for reports.

Ethics, Inclusive AI & Institutional Governance

Bias traps, Khmer-first inclusive design, prompt libraries, audit trails, and Cambodia AI governance framework.
